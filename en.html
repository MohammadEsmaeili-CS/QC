Before LabTrix

In the laboratory, reliable results never happen by accident.

Every result depends on careful recording, checks, and quality control. That takes time and attention.

But the tools we had did not always make that work easier.

Inspired by real laboratory experience
01/05
LABORATORY CHRONICLESSHIRAZ
01Paper and pen

It all started with paper and pen.

We wrote down QC results by hand, used calculators for the numbers, and searched through stacks of files whenever we needed earlier records.

Too much time went into work that should have been simpler.

Handwritten records · Manual calculations · Paper archives
02A step forward

Then came spreadsheets.
Still not quite enough.

Spreadsheets and formulas helped, but we still had to manage files, calculations, and charts separately.

We needed software made for laboratory QC, not just another set of files.

Scattered files · Repeated manual work · Need for a dedicated tool
03The search

We looked for a better tool.

We tried different QC applications. Some did not work well on our computers; others turned basic tasks into a maze of menus and steps.

We wanted to save time, not spend it learning complicated menus.

Compatibility issues · Crowded interfaces · Difficult workflows
04Another obstacle

Solutions existed.
But they were hard to afford.

We contacted software providers and asked about their products. Some options were simply too expensive for our laboratory.

Eventually we bought one, hoping we had finally found the answer.

High prices · Limited options · Higher expectations
05The disappointment

A new purchase.
An outdated experience.

We were using modern versions of Windows, but the software looked and felt years behind. The interface was busy, and finding what we needed took too long.

Why should software designed to simplify our work make it harder?

Dated design · Confusing navigation · Lost time
“
The question that changed everything

If we use a tool every day,
shouldn’t it be easy to work with?

That question changed the direction of our story.

06The turning point

One day I thought:
“Why not build it myself?”

I had years of laboratory experience and knew how to program. I decided to create software from the perspective of someone who would use it every day.

✳I was not trying to create just another application;
I wanted to solve a real problem.
✳ A new beginning

And that is how
LabTrix was born.

It started with the everyday challenges of laboratory work and a clear goal: precise quality control should not require complicated software.
